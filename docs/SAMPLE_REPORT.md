## Leading opportunity

### Evidence-backed knowledge retrieval and response drafting

Suggest relevant approved content and an editable draft response for common
support requests, with clear links to supporting evidence.

**Why it matters:** Specialists repeatedly search multiple systems and rewrite
common explanations. This creates a credible opportunity to reduce search and
drafting effort while improving consistency.

**Required safeguards:**

- Show supporting sources with every recommendation.
- Require specialist review and editing before a response is sent.
- Keep customer data within approved systems.
- Route high-priority and security-related requests to mandatory human review.
- Begin with a narrow set of repetitive request types.

**What still needs validation:**

- Baseline first-response time and search effort.
- Integration options for the knowledge base, tickets, and documentation.
- Knowledge ownership and content-freshness controls.
- A quality benchmark for evidence-supported drafts.

**Suggested pilot measures:** First-response time, search time, draft acceptance
and edit rates, source-support accuracy, customer satisfaction, and the incidence
of outdated or unsupported recommendations.

## Interpretation note

The composite score is a weighted decision aid based on business value,
feasibility, evidence strength, and risk readiness. It is not predicted ROI, a
confidence percentage, or approval to implement.
